Concrete Removal in Columbia, MO

Slabs, driveways, sidewalks, patios, footings and foundations. Broken out, hauled off, recycled, and poured back if you want it.

We break out and haul off concrete. Slabs, driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, footings, retaining walls, and the foundations left behind after a teardown. Our Cat 308 with a hydraulic breaker does the breaking, a grapple does the loading, and the loads go to a concrete recycler in Boone County, not a landfill. If you want it poured back, our concrete crew does that on the same contract. One contractor from the first saw cut to the last broom finish.

What we remove

  • Driveways and aprons. Saw-cut clean at the garage slab and the city sidewalk, broken, hauled. Widening and re-pours quoted on the same proposal.
  • Slabs. Garage floors, shop floors, shed pads, old basketball courts, mobile home pads. Four inch to eight inch, reinforced or not.
  • Sidewalks and patios. Including the ones poured against the house that now drain toward it.
  • Footings and foundations. Block or poured walls and footings left after a house or garage comes down, and the basement backfill that follows. See site work after demolition.
  • Retaining walls. Poured, block or railroad-tie walls that have leaned past saving.
  • Pool decks and pool shells. Decks removed with the shell broken and buried or hauled. See pool removal in Columbia.
  • Commercial flatwork. Loading docks, dumpster pads, sidewalks and small lots. Full parking lots have their own page: parking lot removal and replacement.

What's included

  • Utility locates through Missouri 811 before we break anything
  • Saw-cutting where the removal meets concrete that stays, so the edge is straight and the neighbor slab is not cracked
  • Breaking, loading and hauling with our own equipment and trucks
  • Rebar and wire mesh cut out and hauled with the concrete
  • Recycling at a crushing facility, billed at actual tonnage
  • Sub-grade left rough-graded and compacted, ready for a new pour, gravel, or topsoil and seed

What's not included

  • Concrete leveling, mudjacking, crack filling or patch repair. We don't do it. If a slab can be saved with those, we'll tell you and you can call someone who does.
  • Small stand-alone jobs under a couple thousand dollars that aren't next to other work we're doing. The excavator and trucks cost the same to show up for 80 square feet as they do for 800.
  • Underground tanks, hazardous material, or contaminated soil. If we find it, we stop and talk.

How concrete removal pricing works

You get a fixed price for mobilization, equipment and crew. Disposal is billed as a pass-through at actual loads and tonnage, with the recycler's tickets attached to the invoice. Nothing is marked up on the dump side. Typical Mid-Missouri ranges:

JobTypical range
Driveway, 4-inch unreinforced, 600 to 800 sq ft$2,500 to $5,000
Driveway or slab, 6-inch with rebar or mesh$4 to $8 per sq ft
Sidewalk or patio$3 to $6 per sq ft
Foundation walls and footings after a teardown$4,000 to $12,000
Retaining wall, per linear foot, depending on height$40 to $120
Disposal and recycling, pass-through$40 to $90 per ton

Thickness, reinforcement, how close the excavator can get, and the distance to the recycler are what move these numbers. A four-inch patio with a straight shot from the street is the bottom of the range. An eight-inch reinforced shop floor behind a fence with a 30-inch gate is the top. A site visit gets you a firm number. Read more in our concrete removal cost guide.

How the job goes

  • Site visit. We measure, check thickness at an edge, look at access and where the trucks stage.
  • Written proposal. Fixed price for the removal, disposal as pass-through, and the pour-back option priced separately if you want it.
  • Locates. Missouri 811 is called before we cut. Private lines (irrigation, low-voltage, a line to the shop) are the owner's to flag, and we'll walk it with you.
  • Saw-cut, break, load, haul. Most driveways are one day. A foundation after a teardown is two to three.
  • Grade. Sub-grade rough-graded and compacted. If a new pour is coming, we set the base for it.

We put it back

Most people who call about removing a driveway need a driveway when we leave. Our concrete crew pours it back under our contract, with our warranty and their certificate of insurance on file. Base rock, compaction, forms, reinforcement, a proper pour and control joints, broom finish, and seven days of cure before you park on it. See driveway removal and replacement for how that works and what it costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to remove a concrete driveway in Columbia, MO?

A standard two-car driveway of 600 to 800 square feet at four inches thick runs $2,500 to $5,000 to remove, including hauling and recycling. Six-inch reinforced concrete pushes that to $4 to $8 per square foot because it breaks slower and weighs more. The two things that move the price most are reinforcement and access. If the excavator can sit on the driveway and the trucks can back right up to it, you're at the low end.

Do you haul it all away, and how is disposal billed?

Yes. Every load leaves in our trucks and goes to a concrete recycler. Disposal is billed as a pass-through at the recycler's actual per-ton rate, currently $40 to $90 a ton depending on the facility and whether the concrete is clean. The scale tickets come with the invoice. We don't mark up the dump side, so there's no reason to guess low on tonnage in the bid.

Do you recycle the concrete?

Yes, almost all of it. Clean concrete goes to a crushing facility in the Columbia area where it becomes base rock for the next project, sometimes ours. It's cheaper per ton than landfill and the recycler doesn't care about rebar as long as it's cut short. Concrete with a lot of soil, asphalt or wood mixed in costs more to dump, which is why we keep loads sorted on site.

Will you pour new concrete after the removal?

Yes. Our concrete crew pours driveways, patios, sidewalks, pads and slabs under our contract and warranty. It's quoted on the same proposal as the removal, so you have one price for the whole job and one company to call. If you'd rather use your own finisher, that's fine too. We'll leave the sub-grade compacted and ready for their forms.

Can you remove concrete without damaging the slab next to it?

That's what the saw is for. We cut a straight line where the removal meets concrete that stays, whether that's your garage floor, the city sidewalk or a neighbor's driveway, and break away from the cut. The breaker never touches the side you're keeping. On thin slabs we cut full depth; on thick reinforced slabs we cut deep enough to control the fracture and finish the rebar by hand.

How long does a concrete removal take?

A driveway, patio or sidewalk is usually one day on site. A foundation and footings left after a house teardown is two to three days including backfill. Parking lots are phased and take a week or more. Weather matters less than people think; we can break and haul in most conditions, but we won't run trucks across a soft yard and leave you ruts.

Do you handle utility locates?

Yes. We call Missouri 811 before any cutting or breaking, and we wait the required time for the public utilities to mark. Private lines aren't covered by 811: irrigation, landscape lighting, a buried line to a shop or a well. We walk the site with you before we start so those get flagged. If we hit something nobody knew about, we stop and call you before going further.

How far from Columbia do you travel for concrete removal?

About 45 minutes in any direction: Jefferson City, Fulton, Moberly, Fayette, Boonville, Ashland and the towns between. Mobilization is part of the fixed price, so a small job 40 minutes out costs more per square foot than the same job across town. Larger jobs are worth the drive anywhere in Mid-Missouri.
