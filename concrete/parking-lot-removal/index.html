Parking Lot Removal and Replacement in Columbia, MO

Asphalt or concrete. Taken out in phases so your customers still have somewhere to park, hauled off, and put back with a base that will last.

Old lots crack, heave, hold water and drain the wrong way. We take them out, asphalt or concrete, and put a new one back: base, grade, drainage, surface, curbs and striping. The removal is our excavator and our trucks. The new surface is our concrete or asphalt crew under our contract and warranty. We phase the work so tenants and customers keep a place to park, and you deal with one company from the first saw cut to the last stripe.

Who this is for

Property owners and managers of retail strips, offices, churches, apartment complexes, schools, shops and light-industrial buildings, with lots from about 5,000 to 60,000 square feet. We sell owner-direct. If you're a general contractor with a lot in a larger scope, we'll bid that too. This is not a patch-and-seal service. If your lot needs crack fill and a sealcoat, a paving maintenance company will do that cheaper than we will.

What's included

  • Saw-cut and phased removal of the asphalt or concrete surface
  • Curb, gutter, island, wheel stop, sign base and light pole base removal as needed
  • Base evaluation: reuse it, re-grade it, or replace it. This is the decision that decides whether the new lot lasts
  • Hauling and recycling, billed at actual tonnage. Asphalt millings and clean concrete are both recycled
  • Replacement: new or reworked gravel base, compaction, concrete or asphalt surface, curbs, ADA stalls and ramps where required, striping
  • Drainage correction as part of the regrade, so water leaves the lot instead of sitting in it
  • Traffic control and phasing plan so part of the lot stays open

What's not included

  • Sealcoating, crack filling, pothole patching, or overlay on top of a failing base
  • Underground storm structures, detention basins, or utility relocation beyond what we uncover in the removal. If the regrade needs new inlets, we'll price them, but that's a separate line
  • Lots under roughly $10,000 in total scope. Mobilization for this kind of work doesn't pencil below that

Fixed price for removal labor and equipment. Disposal billed as pass-through at actual tonnage. Replacement priced per square foot on the same proposal, broken out so you can see removal, base and surface separately. Typical Mid-Missouri ranges:

The swing is almost entirely the base. A lot with a sound base that just needs the surface replaced costs about half of one whose base has turned to mud and has to be dug out and rebuilt. We core or dig a test hole on the site visit so the proposal is built on what's actually under there.

Asphalt or concrete?

Asphalt is cheaper up front, faster to open, and easier to patch later. It needs sealcoating every few years and a new surface in 15 to 20. Concrete costs roughly twice as much to install, takes a week to cure before traffic, and then mostly gets left alone for 30 years. Dumpster pads, drive lanes with truck traffic and drainage swales are usually concrete even in an asphalt lot. We'll price it both ways if you want to see the difference.

How the job goes

  • Walk-through. Count spaces, check drainage, find the low spots, test the base.
  • Phasing plan. Drawn on the lot map. Usually two or three phases so half the lot stays open. Overnight and weekend work is available for busy retail.
  • Written proposal. Removal, base, surface, curbs and striping as separate lines. Fixed price except disposal.
  • Removal by phase. Saw-cut, break or mill, load, haul. Base evaluated as it's exposed.
  • Base and grade. Rock added or reworked, compacted, graded to drain.
  • Surface. Poured or paved by our crew. Curbs and ramps formed. Cure time observed.
  • Striping and close-out. Stalls, ADA, arrows, fire lane. Cones come down.

Keeping the lot open

Most owners can't close for two weeks. We split the lot into phases, keep the entrance and the front rows open, and put down temporary striping so the open half functions. For a church that means working Monday through Friday and clearing out for Sunday. For a restaurant it can mean removal at night. Tell us how the property is used and we'll build the phasing around it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to remove a parking lot?

Asphalt removal runs $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot in Mid-Missouri; concrete runs $3 to $6. A 15,000 square foot asphalt lot, about 40 spaces, is $22,000 to $52,000 to tear out, haul and recycle. Curbs, islands and light bases add to that. Disposal is a pass-through at $40 to $90 a ton. Replacement is priced separately on the same proposal so you can see each piece.

Should I replace with asphalt or concrete?

Asphalt is about half the cost to install, opens in a day or two, and needs a sealcoat every few years and a new surface in 15 to 20. Concrete costs more, needs about a week of cure before traffic, and then mostly gets left alone for 30 years. Heavy-truck lanes, dumpster pads and drainage swales should be concrete regardless. We price both ways on request.

Can you keep part of the lot open during the work?

Yes. Almost every lot we do is phased. We split it into two or three sections, keep the entrance and the front rows open, and put down temporary striping so the open section works. Churches get cleared out for Sunday. Retail can be done overnight. Tell us how the property is used and the phasing plan is built around that.

How long does a parking lot replacement take?

Removal on a 15,000 square foot lot is three to five working days across the phases. Base work is one to two days per phase. Asphalt is paved in a day per phase and open the next; concrete needs about seven days of cure before cars and longer before trucks. Plan on two to three weeks start to finish for asphalt and three to four for concrete, with part of the lot open the whole time.

Do you handle ADA stalls and striping?

Yes. New lots get accessible stalls, access aisles, ramps and signage laid out to the current ADA standard, and we stripe stalls, arrows, fire lanes and no-parking zones. If the old lot was short on accessible spaces, replacing it is when that gets fixed. The count depends on total stalls, and we lay it out on the phasing plan so you can see it before we pour.

Is the old asphalt recycled?

Yes. Asphalt comes out as millings or broken slab and goes back to the plant or a recycler, where it becomes new mix or base material. Clean concrete goes to a crusher and comes back as base rock. Both are cheaper per ton than a landfill, and both are billed as pass-through on the invoice with the tickets attached.

Are you insured for commercial work?

Yes. We carry general liability with umbrella coverage and workers' compensation, and every subcontractor on the job has a certificate of insurance on file with us before they start. We'll send certificates naming your property or management company as additional insured before mobilization. Licensed and insured, and the paperwork is ready when you ask.

Do you do parking lots in Jefferson City, Fulton and Moberly?

Yes. Lot work travels well because the job is large enough to cover the mobilization. We regularly work in Jefferson City, Fulton, Moberly, Fayette, Boonville and Ashland, and anywhere else in Mid-Missouri within about 45 minutes of Columbia. Farther out is fine for a lot of this size.
