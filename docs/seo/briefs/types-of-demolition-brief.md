# Research Brief: Types of Demolition

Prepared 2026-11-04. Built from the LaBonne's "Types of Apples" pattern: one page that owns a national informational head term the incumbents have left thin, packaged as a chart + quick picks + per-type sections, written by the business that does the work.

## Why this post

- "types of demolition" / "demolition methods" / "types of demolition techniques" have no authoritative US contractor-written result. Top 10 is an Australian contractor (~700 words), a UK contractor (~900 words, 2020), an engineering-textbook page (2,800 words on thermic lances and bursting, nothing a property owner can use), two issuu PDFs, and a design-build glossary.
- Nobody in the SERP has a comparison chart, cost ranges, or a "which one do I need" decision block.
- This is the authority play. It will not produce leads directly. It pulls links, AI-overview citations, and image-search traffic that lift every geo-modified cost post on the domain.

## Final keyword

- Primary: **types of demolition**
- Secondary (H2/H3): demolition methods, selective demolition, mechanical demolition, interior demolition, deconstruction vs demolition, total demolition, concrete demolition
- Secondary (body/FAQ): demolition techniques, partial demolition, implosion, manual demolition, which type of demolition do I need, cheapest type of demolition

## Search intent

Informational, early-stage. Searcher is a property owner, investor, or GC trying to name what they need before they call anyone. Give them the vocabulary, the cost band for each, and the answer to "which one is mine."

## Structure (follow in order)

1. **Hook** (Chris, first person, "I get this question every week" formula). Searcher usually asks "do I need a full demo or just part of it?"
2. **Quick Answer box.** Name the 5 types a property owner will actually hire (mechanical/total, selective/partial, interior/strip-out, deconstruction, concrete demolition), one line each, and say the other methods (implosion, induced collapse, manual-only) are for a different scale of building.
3. **Types of Demolition Chart** (H2, anchor `#demolition-types-chart`). Table columns: Type | What it is | Typical cost band | Timeline | Best for. Every type name in the table is an anchor link to its H3 below. This is the LaBonne's sweetness chart.
4. **Quick picks: what are you tearing down?** (H2). Situation → type → anchor link:
   - Whole house or mobile home → Mechanical (total) demolition
   - Garage, shed, barn, deck → Mechanical (total) demolition
   - One wing, addition, or attached garage → Selective (partial) demolition
   - Renovation down to studs → Interior demolition (strip-out)
   - Old barn with good timber, historic house → Deconstruction
   - Driveway, slab, pool, foundation, parking lot → Concrete demolition
5. **The types, one H3 each** (H2: "The Types of Demolition, One at a Time"). Each H3 gets: a one-word label line under it (the way LaBonne's puts "Balanced (Sweet-Tart)" under each variety), 2–3 short paragraphs, and a "Best for:" chip line.
   - Mechanical (total) demolition — label "Most common"
   - Selective (partial) demolition — label "Precision"
   - Interior demolition / strip-out — label "Renovation"
   - Deconstruction — label "Salvage"
   - Concrete demolition — label "Flatwork and foundations"
   - Manual demolition — label "Small scale" (brief)
   - Implosion — label "Not for your house" (brief, 1 paragraph, say plainly it is never used on residential)
   - Induced collapse — label "Rare" (brief)
6. **How to choose** (H2). 5 factors: what's staying, what's attached, materials (pre-1980 asbestos rule), access, what the lot needs to look like after.
7. **What a demolition quote should include** (H2). Short. Links the type to the line items.
8. **Local section** (H2: "What's Different in Columbia and Mid-Missouri"). Boone County vs City of Columbia permit split, pre-1980 asbestos inspection requirement, clay soil and basement backfill, burn rules on rural barns.
9. **FAQ** (H2, 5 H3s, FAQPage schema):
   - What is the most common type of demolition?
   - What is the difference between demolition and deconstruction?
   - What is selective demolition?
   - How do I know which type of demolition I need?
   - What is the cheapest type of demolition?
10. **Need Demolition Done?** soft pitch + CTA to `/consultation/` (primary) and `tel:5732346641`.

**Contextual mid-article CTAs** (2, not the generic box): after the Selective section ("Keeping part of the structure? That's the walkthrough call, and it's free"), and after Concrete demolition ("Driveway or lot coming out? See `/concrete/concrete-removal/`").
