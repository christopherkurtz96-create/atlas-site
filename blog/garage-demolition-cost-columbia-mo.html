Garage Demolition in Columbia, MO: Cost, Process & Permits

What it costs to tear down a garage in Mid-Missouri — and what to expect from start to finish.

March 23, 2026 5 min read

Whether it's leaning, rotting, or simply in the way of new plans for your property, tearing down an old garage is one of the most common demolition projects we handle in Columbia, MO. But before you start, it helps to know what it costs, how the process works, and whether you need a permit.

This guide covers everything Mid-Missouri homeowners need to know about garage demolition — from cost breakdowns by garage type to the step-by-step process and Columbia-specific permit requirements.

How Much Does Garage Demolition Cost in Columbia, MO?

Most residential garage demolitions in the Columbia area cost between $2,000 and $8,000. The price depends on the garage's size, construction material, whether it's attached to your home, and whether you need the foundation removed.

Garage Type Typical Cost Range
Detached garage (1-car, wood) $1,500 – $3,000
Detached garage (2-car, wood) $2,500 – $4,500
Detached garage (concrete/block) $3,000 – $5,500
Attached garage (1-car) $3,500 – $5,000
Attached garage (2-car) $4,500 – $8,000
Foundation/slab removal (add-on) $700 – $1,500

On a per-square-foot basis, expect to pay $3 to $5 per square foot for a detached garage and $8 to $10 per square foot for an attached garage in the Columbia area. If you need the concrete slab removed, our concrete removal team can handle that as part of the project.

These are Columbia-area estimates — your actual cost depends on your specific project. Factors like asbestos in older garages, difficult site access, or extra grading work common with Missouri's clay soil can push costs higher. For full house demolition pricing, see our house demolition cost guide for Columbia, MO.

Cost tip: The cheapest bid isn't always the best value. Make sure your estimate includes debris hauling, disposal fees, and site cleanup — not just the teardown. Some contractors quote low and add these as extras later.

Attached vs. Detached Garage Demolition

The biggest factor in garage demolition cost is whether the garage is attached or detached from your home. Attached garages cost roughly twice as much to demolish because of the additional work involved.

Detached Garage Demolition

A freestanding garage is the simplest demolition project. There's no shared wall with your home, no risk to your house's structure, and no utility lines to reroute. Most detached garage demos are completed in 1 to 2 days including debris removal and cleanup.

Attached Garage Demolition

Tearing down an attached garage is more complex. Your contractor needs to assess the shared wall structure, protect your home's framing, reroute any electrical or plumbing lines that run through the garage, and seal and finish the new exterior wall. Expect 2 to 4 days for the full project.

Important: Attached garage demolition requires protecting your home's structure — always hire a qualified demolition contractor for this type of project.

The Garage Demolition Process: What to Expect

Whether your garage is attached or detached, the demolition process follows the same general steps. Here's what to expect when you hire a contractor for garage demolition in Columbia, MO.

  1. Get estimates — Contact 2 to 3 demolition contractors for written quotes. Ask about insurance, what's included, and who handles permits. Need help comparing? Use our checklist for hiring a demolition contractor.
  2. Permits and inspections — Your contractor pulls the demolition permit from Columbia's Building & Site Development department. For a detailed walkthrough, read our Columbia demolition permit guide.
  3. Utility disconnection — All electrical, gas, and water lines running to the garage are disconnected before any work begins.
  4. Hazardous material check — Garages built before 1980 may contain asbestos siding, insulation, or lead paint. Missouri law requires certified removal of asbestos-containing materials, and your contractor should coordinate an inspection if needed.
  5. Demolition — Heavy equipment tears down the structure. A typical detached garage comes down in a few hours.
  6. Debris removal and cleanup — All materials are loaded and hauled to approved disposal sites. The job site is left clean.
  7. Site restoration — Grading, backfill, or site preparation for new construction or landscaping.

Do You Need a Permit to Demolish a Garage in Columbia?

Yes. Within Columbia city limits, you need a demolition permit to tear down a garage or any other structure. The permit is issued by the City of Columbia's Building & Site Development department at (573) 874-7474.

If your property is in unincorporated Boone County, permit requirements may differ. Contact Boone County Resource Management for guidance on your specific situation.

A reputable contractor handles all permitting on your behalf. If a contractor asks you to pull the permits, that's a red flag when hiring a demolition contractor. For more on Columbia's permit process, see our complete demolition permit guide.

DIY vs. Professional Garage Demolition

It's tempting to rent a dumpster and grab a sledgehammer, but DIY garage demolition comes with real risks:

  • Safety hazards — structural collapse, falling debris, and power tool injuries
  • Asbestos exposure — older garages may contain hazardous materials that require certified removal
  • Improper disposal fines — Missouri has strict regulations about where demolition debris can go, and violating state disposal rules can result in penalties
  • Hidden costs — equipment rental ($500+), dumpster fees ($400–$800), and disposal charges add up fast

A professional demolition contractor carries insurance, handles permits, completes the work in a day or two, and ensures proper disposal. For anything beyond a small wood shed, professional demolition is safer and often more cost-effective than doing it yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to demolish a garage in Columbia, MO?

Most garage demolitions in the Columbia area cost between $1,500 and $8,000. Detached wood garages are the least expensive ($1,500–$3,000 for a one-car), while attached two-car garages are the most expensive ($4,500–$8,000). Foundation removal adds $700 to $1,500.

Do I need a permit to tear down my garage in Columbia?

Yes. Columbia requires a demolition permit for any structure, including garages. Your demolition contractor should handle the permit process for you through the City's Building & Site Development department.

How long does garage demolition take?

A detached garage typically takes 1 to 2 days from demolition through cleanup. Attached garages take 2 to 4 days because of the additional structural work needed to protect and repair your home's exterior wall.

Is it cheaper to demolish an attached or detached garage?

Detached garages are significantly cheaper — roughly half the cost of an attached garage. Attached garages require structural assessment, shared wall repair, weatherproofing, and utility rerouting, which adds to labor and material costs.

Can I demolish my own garage, or do I need a contractor?

While it's technically possible to tear down a small detached wood garage yourself, professional demolition is strongly recommended. Contractors carry insurance, handle permits, manage hazardous material risks, and complete the work safely. The hidden costs of DIY (equipment rental, dumpster, disposal fees) often rival professional pricing.
