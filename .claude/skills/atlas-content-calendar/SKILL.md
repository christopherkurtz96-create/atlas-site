## Brand Context — Atlas Excavation & Demolition

**What we do:** Structural demolition, excavation, and site prep for property owners and general contractors in Mid-Missouri. We handle the entire process — permits, hazmat coordination, demo, debris hauling, and site grading — so the customer's only job is telling us when to start.

**Service area:** Columbia, MO — approximately 45-60 minute radius (Boone County, Callaway County, Cole County, Audrain County, and surrounding areas)

**Owner:** Chris Kurtz

**Core offers:**
- **The Atlas Complete Demo Package** — Full structural demolition from permits to clean site
- **The Atlas Site-Ready Package** — Excavation, grading, and site prep to spec

**Primary avatars:**
1. Property owners (residential) — First-time demo buyers, nervous about cost and process, age 35-65
2. Property owners (commercial) — Investors/developers, care about timeline, insurance, permits
3. General contractors — Need reliable demo/excavation subs who show up and communicate
4. Realtors / Property managers — Need a contractor they can confidently refer

**Voice:** Blue-collar professional. Direct. Honest. No-BS. Confident but not cocky. Sounds like a contractor who also knows how to run a business.

**Core differentiator:** "We answer the phone, show up when we say, and leave the site clean. In this industry, that's rare."

## Content Pillars (All Content Must Serve One)

1. **"We Answer The Phone"** — Reliability and communication. The #1 frustration in the industry is contractors who ghost. Every piece showing responsiveness reinforces Atlas's core differentiator.
2. **"Before & After"** — Visual proof. Demolished structures, cleared lots, graded sites. Nothing sells demo and excavation like dramatic transformation photos and videos.
3. **"No Surprises"** — Transparent pricing, clear communication, honest timelines. Content that educates on what things cost and why — positions Atlas as the trustworthy expert.
4. **"Behind The Iron"** — Equipment and process content. Show the machines working, explain what's happening and why, demystify the process. Builds perceived expertise.
5. **"Mid-Missouri Strong"** — Local pride. Columbia-area projects, community connection, local landmarks and properties. Reinforces service area and builds local identity.

## Platform Posting Specs Quick Reference

### Facebook (Primary)
- Text: 300 characters before truncation; short stories and direct address perform best
- Video: 15 sec-3 min; captions required; demo footage = gold
- Photo: Real job photos only. Square or vertical. Before/after side-by-side performs highest
- Optimal posting: Mon-Wed, 9am-1pm CT; Wed highest engagement
- Hashtags: 1-3 only (#ColumbiaExcavation #MidMissouri #DemolitionDay)
- CTA: Can link directly in caption; phone number in every conversion post
- Audience: Property owners 35-65, local community, some GCs

### Instagram (Primary — Visual)
- Caption: 125 characters before fold
- Reels: 15-60 sec; demo footage with dramatic moments (first hit, collapse, clean site reveal)
- Carousel: Before → during → after sequences. Up to 10 slides
- Optimal posting: Mon 3-9pm CT; Tue-Fri morning
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant (#demolition #excavation #columbiamo #siteprep #beforeandafter #heavyequipment)
- CTA: "Link in bio" or "Call/text [number]"

### Google Business Profile (Critical for Local SEO)
- Post weekly: project highlight, tip, or seasonal offer
- Image: 1080 × 1080 min, real photo
- Text: 100-300 words; front-load key point
- CTA button: "Call Now" or "Get Quote"
- Upload 2-3 new photos per week (geo-tagged)

### LinkedIn (Secondary — GC-Focused)
- Text posts: Professional tone, industry insight
- Hook under 35 words before "see more"
- Target: GCs, property managers, commercial developers
- Hashtags: 3-5 (#ExcavationContractor #CommercialDemolition #MidMissouriConstruction)

## Three-Phase Content Structure

**Phase 1 — Awareness (Weeks 1-4)**
Goal: Show Atlas exists, build visual proof library, expand local reach
Mix: 50% Before/After, 25% Behind The Iron, 15% Mid-Missouri Strong, 10% education
Hook types: Visual transformation, curiosity gap, specificity
CTA focus: Follow, save, share, visit website
Pillars emphasized: Before & After, Mid-Missouri Strong

**Phase 2 — Engagement (Weeks 5-8)**
Goal: Build trust, drive conversation, generate estimate requests
Mix: 30% Before/After, 25% No Surprises (education), 25% Behind The Iron, 20% engagement
Hook types: Direct address, story opening, question
CTA focus: Comment, tag a friend, share with someone who needs this
Pillars emphasized: No Surprises, We Answer The Phone

**Phase 3 — Conversion (Weeks 9-12)**
Goal: Drive estimate requests, phone calls, and referral partner connections
Mix: 30% Before/After with CTA, 25% No Surprises, 25% social proof/reviews, 20% offers
Hook types: Payoff preview, specificity, social proof
CTA focus: Call now, request estimate, DM for quote
Pillars emphasized: We Answer The Phone, No Surprises

## Engagement Instructions — Standard Template

```
Golden Hour: Reply to all comments within 60 min. Thank and engage genuinely.
Proactive: Comment on 3-5 posts from local businesses, GCs, realtors, and community pages.
Watch for: Any question about demo cost, excavation, site prep, or "who do you recommend" → treat as warm lead.
Avoid: Generic emoji replies, self-promotional comments on others' posts.
Voice: Friendly, professional, blue-collar honest. Like a contractor who's proud of his work.
```
