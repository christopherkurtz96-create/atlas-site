# Atlas Excavation & Demolition Engagement Response Skill
## Comment Strategy for Algorithmic Boost and Lead Generation

---

## Brand Context

**Atlas Excavation & Demolition** handles structural demolition, excavation, and site prep for property owners and GCs in Mid-Missouri (Columbia, MO — 45-60 minute radius).

**Voice in comments:** Friendly, direct, blue-collar professional. Sounds like a contractor you'd trust with your property. Not salesy, not corporate. Like Chris answering a question at a barbecue.

**Core differentiator to reinforce in every interaction:** We answer the phone, show up when we say, and leave the site clean.

## Why This Matters

| Platform | Engagement Lift from Replying |
|---|---|
| Facebook | Meaningful lift in distribution; comments drive thread visibility |
| Instagram | +21% reach; first-hour replies determine Explore push |
| LinkedIn | +30% |

**Critical window:** Reply within 60-90 minutes of posting. This is when platforms decide how widely to distribute your content.

For a local service business, every comment interaction is also a potential lead. Someone asking "how much does demo cost?" in your comments is a warm prospect.

---

## Comment Triage System

**Tier 1 — HIGH PRIORITY (respond within 30 minutes)**
- Questions about cost, process, timeline, or availability
- "Do you serve [city]?" — service area questions
- Personal project descriptions ("I have an old barn that needs to come down")
- GCs asking about subcontracting or availability
- Realtors or property managers inquiring
- Negative feedback or complaints (rare but critical)
- Tags from other accounts ("@atlas you should talk to this guy")

**Tier 2 — MEDIUM PRIORITY (respond within 60 minutes)**
- Genuine reactions ("That's awesome" / "Wow that was fast")
- People tagging friends ("@john you need to call these guys")
- Compliments on specific work shown in the post
- Questions about equipment or process ("What machine is that?")

**Tier 3 — LOW PRIORITY (respond within 24 hours)**
- Generic praise ("Nice!" / fire emoji)
- Regulars who always engage
- Non-lead general comments

**Skip:** Spam, bots, off-topic promotional comments

## Reply Frameworks by Comment Type

### Cost/Pricing Question
**Structure:** Honest answer with ranges → explain what drives cost → invite to get specific estimate

Example:
> Comment: "How much does it cost to tear down a house?"
> Reply: "Depends on a few things — size, material (wood frame vs. concrete), whether there's asbestos that needs abatement first, and access for equipment. For a typical residential house demo in our area, you're usually looking at $8K-$20K all-in including debris hauling and site cleanup. Best way to know for sure is a site visit — we do those free and get you a detailed estimate within 72 hours. Give us a call at [number] or shoot us a message."

### "Do You Serve [City]?" Question
**Structure:** Direct answer → confirm or explain service area → invite next step

Example:
> Comment: "Do you guys come out to Fulton?"
> Reply: "We do — Fulton is right in our service area. We cover about a 45-minute radius from Columbia. What've you got going on? Happy to come take a look and get you an estimate."

### Personal Project Description
**Structure:** Acknowledge their situation → show you understand → make it easy to take the next step

Example:
> Comment: "I've got an old mobile home on my property that's been sitting there for years. The county keeps sending me letters."
> Reply: "We get that call a lot — and those county letters are no fun. Mobile home removal is straightforward for us. We handle the demo, haul everything off, and leave the lot clean. Shoot us a call at [number] or DM us and we can set up a time to come look at it. Usually have estimates back within a couple days."

### GC Inquiry
**Structure:** Professional, peer-to-peer → reliability focus → open the door

Example:
> Comment: "Looking for a reliable demo sub in the Columbia area. Tired of getting ghosted."
> Reply: "That's the story we hear from every GC we work with — it's unfortunately the norm in this industry. We show up when we say, communicate throughout the job, and leave the site ready for your next trade. Shoot me a DM or call [number] — happy to talk about what you've got coming up."

### Realtor/PM Inquiry
**Structure:** Acknowledge their role → reassure their referral is safe → make it easy

Example:
> Comment: "I'm a realtor and I have a client who needs a structure removed before listing. Do you handle that?"
> Reply: "Absolutely — we work with several realtors in the area for exactly this. We'll treat your client like our own, communicate directly, and get it done fast so your listing timeline stays on track. DM me or call [number] and I'll get you connected."

### Process/Equipment Question
**Structure:** Answer genuinely → explain briefly → use it as a teaching moment

Example:
> Comment: "What size excavator is that?"
> Reply: "That's our CAT 320 — perfect for residential and light commercial demo. Big enough to handle a full house teardown but nimble enough to work in tight spaces without tearing up the yard more than necessary. On bigger commercial jobs we bring in heavier iron."

### Negative Feedback
**Structure:** Acknowledge → don't argue publicly → take it offline

Example:
> Comment: "You guys left debris on the edge of my property last month."
> Reply: "That's not our standard, and I'm sorry that happened. Can you call me directly at [number]? I want to make sure we get this taken care of for you right away."

### Generic Praise
**Structure:** Thank genuinely → reference something specific → keep it warm

Example:
> Comment: "Nice work!"
> Reply: "Appreciate that! This one was a fun job — 1960s house in Ashland, took about 3 days from first swing to clean lot. The before/after on this one was dramatic."

### Friend Tag ("@friend you need this")
**Structure:** Welcome the tagged person → make it easy for them

Example:
> Comment: "@mike you should call these guys about that old barn"
> Reply: "Mike — whenever you're ready, give us a call at [number] or shoot us a DM. Happy to come take a look and give you a straight answer on what it'd take."

## Platform-Specific Rules

### Facebook (Primary)
- Reply within 60 minutes — this is where most Atlas leads come from
- Every reply should feel like a conversation, not a customer service response
- Warm and direct. Use the person's first name
- For lead questions: Always include phone number AND "DM us" as options
- Comment threads drive Facebook distribution — longer threads = more reach
- Comment on local community pages, contractor groups, and real estate groups proactively

### Instagram
- Reply within 2 hours of posting, especially on Reels
- First-hour engagement determines Explore distribution
- Shorter replies okay here — but still substantive
- DM invitations work well for lead conversion on Instagram
- Reply to Story replies promptly

### Google Business Profile
- Respond to every review within 24 hours (see SEO skill for review response formulas)
- Answer all Q&A questions — and proactively seed Q&A with common questions

### LinkedIn
- Professional tone but still Atlas-direct
- GC and commercial-focused replies
- Comment on GC posts, commercial developer posts, and construction industry content

## Proactive Engagement (Commenting on Others' Posts)

Before and after posting, engage with 3-5 posts from:
- Local community Facebook groups
- Columbia, MO business pages and community pages
- GC and construction industry pages
- Realtor and property management pages
- Local news posts about development, demolition, or construction

**What makes a proactive comment worth posting:**
- Adds genuine value or local knowledge
- References Atlas experience without being salesy
- "We see that all the time in our work..." or "Good point — from the demo side, I'd add..."
- 2-3 sentences minimum — never just "great post" or emoji

## Atlas Voice in Comments — Examples

**Right:**
- "That's a good question — short answer is it depends on size and material, but I can give you a real number with a site visit."
- "Appreciate you thinking of us. Tell your friend to give us a call — we'll take care of him."
- "We've done a few jobs out in Fulton. Good area. What've you got going on?"
- "Yeah, that's a common issue with older structures. Here's what we usually recommend..."

**Wrong:**
- "Thanks for your interest! Please visit our website for more information! 🎉"
- "We'd LOVE to help you! DM us NOW for a FREE quote!!!"
- "Great question! There are so many factors to consider!"
- Generic copy-paste replies used on multiple comments

---

## Lead Conversion in Comments

When a comment signals buying intent:
1. **Answer their question directly in the public comment** — builds trust with everyone reading
2. **Include the phone number** — make it effortless
3. **Offer DM as an alternative** — some people prefer private
4. **Follow up if they don't respond** — one gentle follow-up in 24-48 hours via DM: "Hey [Name], just wanted to make sure you saw my reply. Happy to answer any other questions about your project."

**Never:** Hard close in public comments. Push urgency that isn't real. Use automated-sounding language.
