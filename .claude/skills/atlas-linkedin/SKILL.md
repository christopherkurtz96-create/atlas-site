#### Post Structure Framework

```markdown
## LinkedIn Post Structure

### The Anatomy of a High-Performing LinkedIn Post

**Line 1 (ABOVE THE FOLD -- This is everything)**:
- Must stop the scroll
- Must compel the click on "...see more"
- Options: Insight, provocative question, contrarian take, surprising stat
- Character limit before fold: ~210 characters on desktop, ~150 on mobile

**Body (Lines 2-8)**:
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Single-sentence lines for emphasis
- White space between sections
- Bold key stats or insights
- Tell a story OR share a framework (not both in same post)

**Closing (Last 2-3 lines)**:
- Key takeaway (one sentence)
- Question or CTA to drive comments
- Make the question EASY to answer thoughtfully

### Formatting Rules
- Break long paragraphs into single sentences with line breaks
- Use bold for statistics and key terms
- No more than 3-4 sentences per paragraph
- Emoji: sparingly and purposefully (not every line)
- No hashtags in first line (looks spammy)
- Limit to 3-5 hashtags at the end (reduced importance in 2026)
```

#### Golden Hour Protocol

```markdown
## Golden Hour Protocol (First 60-90 Minutes)

### 30 Minutes BEFORE Posting
- [ ] Engage with 5-10 posts in your feed (genuine comments, 10+ words)
- [ ] Reply to any pending messages or comments
- [ ] This signals to the algorithm you're "active" and primes distribution

### Immediately After Posting
- [ ] Do NOT leave LinkedIn -- stay active
- [ ] If you have team members or close colleagues, ask 2-3 to engage early

### First 60-90 Minutes After Posting
- [ ] Respond to EVERY comment within minutes
- [ ] Reply with a follow-up question (keeps conversation going = more comments)
- [ ] Each substantive comment thread creates compounding algorithmic value
- [ ] Continue engaging with others' content (algorithm sees active user)

### What Makes a "Substantive" Comment (10+ words)
- Adds a personal experience related to the post topic
- Offers a different perspective with reasoning
- Asks a thoughtful follow-up question
- Shares relevant data or resource
- NOT: "Great post!", "Love this!", "So true!" (low algorithmic weight)
```

#### Comment Strategy (Commenting on OTHERS' Posts)

```markdown
## Comment Strategy for Growth

### Why This Works
When you leave substantive comments on high-visibility posts, your name and headline appear in front of that person's entire network. Great comments drive profile visits and follows.

### Target Account Selection
- Identify 20-30 accounts in your niche with strong engagement
- Mix: industry leaders, peers at similar stage, complementary professionals
- Prioritize accounts whose audience matches YOUR target audience

### Daily Comment Routine (15-20 min)
- [ ] Comment on 5-10 posts daily with 10+ word substantive comments
- [ ] Prioritize posts published in the last 2 hours (you'll be among first comments)
- [ ] Add genuine value: experience, data, alternative perspective, question
- [ ] Engage with other commenters on those posts (builds relationships)

### Comment Formulas
1. "This resonates because [personal experience]. I'd add [additional insight]."
2. "Interesting perspective. In my experience with [context], I've found [different angle]."
3. "[Agreement + expansion]. The part about [specific point] is especially true because [reasoning]."
```

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Posting external links in main post: 60% reach penalty. Keep content native or use link-in-comments.

Generic motivational posts: "Hard work pays off!" is oversaturated. Share SPECIFIC insights from YOUR experience.

Posting and ghosting: If you don't engage in the Golden Hour, the algorithm won't expand your reach. Stay active for 60-90 min after posting.

Quantity over quality: LinkedIn punishes mediocre posts more than any other platform. 3 excellent posts per week beats 7 average ones.

"Like if you agree!" style engagement bait: Gets filtered at the quality gate. Ask genuine questions that invite thoughtful responses.

Using single images: Single-image posts underperform text-only by 30% in 2026. Use multi-image carousels or skip images entirely for text posts.

Corporate jargon: LinkedIn is shifting toward authentic, human conversation. Write like you talk, not like a press release.

Only posting, never commenting: Commenting on others' posts is a major growth lever. Allocate equal time to creating and commenting.
