#### Golden Hour Protocol

```markdown
## Golden Hour Protocol (First 60-90 Minutes After Posting)

### 30 Minutes BEFORE Posting
- [ ] Engage with 5-10 posts in your niche (genuine comments, not spam)
- [ ] Reply to any pending DMs
- [ ] Watch and react to Stories from accounts in your niche

### Immediately After Posting
- [ ] Share post to Stories with commentary or "New post" sticker
- [ ] Send post to 3-5 people via DM who would genuinely find it valuable
- [ ] Post to any relevant community or group

### First 60 Minutes After Posting
- [ ] Respond to EVERY comment within minutes (speed matters)
- [ ] Reply with questions to keep conversations going (drives more comments)
- [ ] Continue engaging with others' content (algorithm sees you as "active")

### Ongoing Daily Engagement (15-20 min/day)
- [ ] Reply to all comments and DMs
- [ ] Comment thoughtfully on 10-15 posts from accounts in your niche
- [ ] Watch and interact with Stories from your community
- [ ] Use DMs for genuine relationship building (algorithm tracks DM interactions)
```

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Scattered topics**: Posting about unrelated themes confuses the algorithm
✅ **Topic clarity**: Last 9-12 posts should form a cohesive niche

❌ **Ignoring Reels**: Reels are THE growth format — no Reels = no discovery
✅ **Reels-first approach**: Even simple, authentic Reels outperform polished images

❌ **30 generic hashtags**: Old strategy, no longer effective
✅ **3-5 niche hashtags** + keyword-rich captions for Instagram SEO

❌ **Posting and ghosting**: Algorithm rewards active engagement around your post
✅ **Golden Hour protocol**: Engage 30 min before, 60 min after every post

❌ **Chasing trends over consistency**: Random viral attempts don't build followers
✅ **Signature series**: Binge-worthy recurring content people come back for

❌ **Reposting others' content**: Aggregator penalty replaces you with the original creator
✅ **Original content**: Authenticity is weighted more heavily than ever

❌ **Long captions with no structure**: Walls of text get scrolled past
✅ **Formatted captions**: Line breaks, short paragraphs, hook above the fold
