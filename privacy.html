Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Atlas Excavation & Demolition ("Atlas," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when using our website (deployatlas.com) and our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a quote request or instant bid form
  • Contact us by phone, email, or web form
  • Subscribe to receive text messages from Atlas

This may include your name, phone number, email address, project address, and details about the work you are requesting.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide estimates
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Send appointment confirmations, project updates, and follow-up messages via SMS, email, or phone
  • Improve our services

SMS / Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and opting in through our website forms, you consent to receive text messages from Atlas Excavation & Demolition related to your estimate, project, scheduling, and follow-up communication.

Message frequency varies and typically ranges from 2 to 6 messages per project.

Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at 573-234-6641.

Mobile Information Sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in data will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors is limited to what is necessary to provide the service you requested (for example, scheduling or job coordination).

How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services (such as form processors, scheduling tools, and communication platforms like Twilio) to operate our business. These providers are bound by their own privacy and security obligations and only access data necessary to deliver their service.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP
  • Request that we delete your information by emailing chris@deployatlas.com
  • Update your contact information by contacting us directly

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
